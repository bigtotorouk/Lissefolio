# Lissefolio Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**

Lissefolio helps you turn documents, images, web pages, and text into a personal listening library. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Local Processing and Storage

Lissefolio does not require an account. Your imported content is processed and stored on your device, rather than uploaded to servers operated by us.

Locally stored information may include:

- Imported PDFs, images, scanned pages, text, and downloaded web content.
- Extracted and edited reading text.
- Playlist titles, segments, and their order.
- Listening progress, playback preferences, and language settings.
- Locally cached Pro entitlement status.

Text extraction, image text recognition, and speech playback use Apple frameworks on your device. Lissefolio does not send your documents to an online AI or text-to-speech service.

## 2. Web Link Imports

When you import a web link, Lissefolio connects directly to the requested website to download supported content. The request may follow redirects.

The website and its service providers may receive your IP address, the requested URL, and standard network request information. Their handling of this information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Lissefolio uses a temporary network session for imports, with cookie storage and persistent network caching disabled. Downloaded content that you save is stored locally in your library.

Both HTTP and HTTPS links are supported. HTTP connections are not encrypted in transit. Avoid importing links containing confidential information or access tokens.

## 3. Permissions and System Sharing

Lissefolio uses access only when needed for a feature you choose:

- **Camera:** To scan paper documents.
- **Photos and files:** To read items you select through system pickers.
- **Share extension:** To receive PDFs and images you explicitly share from another app.

The share extension temporarily stores incoming files in an on-device container shared with the main app so they can be imported.

You can manage camera and other available permissions in iOS Settings.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Lissefolio Pro is purchased through Apple’s App Store.

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your payment card details or billing credentials.

Lissefolio uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to verify purchases, restore purchases, and maintain Pro access. The app stores a local entitlement status for this purpose.

Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Restoring a purchase restores eligible Pro access; it does not restore your documents or playlists.

## 5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Lissefolio does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple may separately process App Store usage, purchase, or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

## 6. Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Lissefolio does not upload your listening library to us or publicly distribute your content. Network imports, Apple services, and external links involve the third parties described in this policy.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved library and playlists remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app’s local data.

Deleting a library document does not delete independent text copies already added to playlists. To remove those copies, delete the corresponding segments or playlists as well.

Files received through the share extension may remain pending until you complete or discard the import.

Deleting the app removes its main local data. Offloading the app may retain documents and settings. System backups, shared extension storage, and copies held outside the app may remain subject to iOS behavior and your backup settings.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your listening library and cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

## 8. Backups and Security

Lissefolio does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups depending on your system settings.

The app relies on iOS storage protections and your device security settings. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Support Communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and attachments.

We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to resolve the issue, handle related follow-up, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Please avoid sending sensitive documents unless necessary. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

Lissefolio does not require users to submit a name, age, or account profile, and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us.

## 11. External Links

Links to websites, legal pages, or Apple support resources are governed by the destination service’s privacy policy. This policy does not cover those services.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
