# Lissefolio Support

Need help with Lissefolio? Find answers below or contact us.

## Importing Content

Lissefolio supports PDFs, images, scanned documents, pasted text, and supported web links.

If an import fails:

- Check that the file is readable and not password-protected.
- Use clear, well-lit images when scanning documents.
- For web imports, use a publicly accessible link. Pages requiring login or JavaScript may not work.
- Web downloads are limited to 20 MB per request. For larger files, download them separately and import through Files.

Complex layouts, tables, and formulas may require manual text correction.

## Voices and Playback

Lissefolio reads text using Apple system voices.

- Choose an available voice in the app’s settings.
- Additional voices can be downloaded through iOS accessibility settings. The app includes instructions for finding them.
- Offline playback depends on the voices installed on your device.
- If you hear no sound, check the media volume and connected headphones or Bluetooth devices.

Lissefolio reads text aloud but does not export audio files.

## Playlists and Editing

You can listen directly from your library or organize content into playlists.

Playlist segments contain independent text copies. Editing a segment does not change the original document or other playlists.

Deleting a library item does not remove copies already saved in playlists. Delete those segments separately if you no longer need them.

## Free and Pro

The free version includes:

- Unlimited listening time.
- Up to 10 library items.
- Up to 2 playlists.
- Up to 10 segments per playlist.

Lissefolio Pro removes these quantity limits through a one-time purchase. Actual storage and performance depend on your device.

## Restoring Pro

Use **Restore Purchases** on the Pro purchase screen while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

If restoration fails, check your internet connection and Apple Account, then try again.

Restoring Pro restores purchase access, not documents or playlists.

For refunds, visit:

https://reportaproblem.apple.com/

## Your Documents

Lissefolio stores your library on your device and does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Deleting the app may remove your saved content. Keep original documents separately before deleting the app or changing devices.

## Report a Problem

Please include:

- Your device model.
- Your iOS version.
- Your Lissefolio version.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- A screenshot, if helpful.

Please remove personal or confidential information from screenshots and sample files.
